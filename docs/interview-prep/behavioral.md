# Behavioral / STAR

Structure every behavioral answer as **STAR** and keep it ~2 minutes.

## The STAR method
- **Situation** — brief context (1–2 sentences).
- **Task** — your specific responsibility/goal.
- **Action** — what **you** did (say "I", not "we"); the bulk of the answer.
- **Result** — the outcome; quantify it (latency, %, time saved, incidents avoided).

## Prepare stories for these themes
Have 5–6 flexible stories you can adapt:
- A hard **technical problem** you solved.
- A **production incident** / bug you diagnosed and fixed.
- **Ownership** — something you drove end-to-end.
- **Conflict / disagreement** and how you resolved it.
- Delivering under **time pressure / ambiguity**.
- A **mistake** you made — and what you changed afterward (accountability).
- **Leadership / mentoring / collaboration**.

## Common questions
- "Tell me about yourself" — 60–90s: role → strengths → why this role.
- "Tell me about a challenging project."
- "A time you disagreed with a teammate/manager."
- "A time you failed / a mistake."
- "How do you handle tight deadlines / competing priorities?"
- "Why do you want this role / leave your current one?" (stay positive).

## Tips
- **Lead with impact**, then explain how.
- **Quantify** wherever possible; be specific, not generic.
- Own your part clearly; give credit without hiding your contribution.
- For failures, emphasize the **fix + prevention**, not blame.
- Prepare **questions to ask** the interviewer (team, challenges, success metrics).

## STAR skeleton to fill in
> **S**: _context_ · **T**: _my goal_ · **A**: _what I did (steps)_ · **R**: _measurable outcome + learning_
