## Interview Approach

### Structure Your Answer
1. **Direct answer**: Clearly state your position
2. **Why**: Explain reasoning and trade-offs
3. **Examples**: Provide code or real-world examples
4. **Alternatives**: Discuss other approaches when relevant

### Tips
- **Clarify**: Rephrase the question to confirm understanding
- **Think aloud**: Explain your thought process
- **Be honest**: "I don't know, but I'd approach it this way..."
- **Follow-ups**: Be ready for deeper questions
- **Connect**: Link concepts to related topics
